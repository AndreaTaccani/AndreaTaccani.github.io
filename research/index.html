Research

Arithmetic geometry and number theory

I am interested in geometric constructions that organize arithmetic information, particularly in p-adic and cohomological settings.

Research directions

I am at the beginning of my research career. These are the questions and tools I am currently studying and using.

Geometry

Poincaré bundles and theta functions

Biextension geometry, theta functions, differential identities, and distribution relations on elliptic curves and abelian varieties.

Cohomology

Eisenstein classes and realizations

Eisenstein–Kronecker and polylogarithmic constructions, together with their motivic, syntomic, and étale realizations.

p-adic theory

Iwasawa theory and special values

Norm-compatible arithmetic classes, p-adic distributions, p-adic L-functions, and special-value formulas.

In progress · 2026

Master's thesis

Poincaré Theta Distributions along Ordinary p-Power Isogenies

The thesis studies theta distributions obtained from the Poincaré bundle along ordinary p-power isogenies. Its scope includes the underlying biextension geometry, distribution relations, and their p-adic interpretation.
