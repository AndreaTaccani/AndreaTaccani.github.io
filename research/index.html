Research

Arithmetic geometry and number theory

My interests concern geometric constructions that produce and organize arithmetic information, especially in p-adic and cohomological settings.

Themes

Current directions

I am at the beginning of my research career. The topics below describe the questions and tools I am currently studying and using.

Geometry

Poincaré bundles and theta functions

I study the Poincaré bundle as a source of theta functions, differential identities, and distribution relations on elliptic curves and, more broadly, on abelian varieties.

Cohomology

Eisenstein classes and realizations

I am interested in Eisenstein–Kronecker and polylogarithmic constructions, together with their motivic, syntomic, and étale realizations.

p-adic theory

Iwasawa theory and special values

A longer-term aim is to understand how geometric cohomology classes and norm-compatible systems relate to p-adic L-functions and special-value formulas.

In progress · 2026

Master's thesis

Poincaré Theta Distributions along Ordinary p-Power Isogenies

The project studies theta distributions obtained from the Poincaré bundle along ordinary p-power isogenies. Its scope includes the underlying biextension geometry, distribution relations, and the passage to p-adic constructions.

The thesis is still in progress. A public manuscript will be linked only after a stable version is available.
