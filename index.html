Arithmetic geometry · Number theory

Andrea Taccani

I am a doctoral researcher in mathematics at the University of Regensburg, working with Prof. Guido Kings.

My research is in arithmetic geometry and number theory. I am particularly interested in p-adic geometry, cohomological constructions, arithmetic distributions, formal groups, and the integral structures that connect them.

Research interests

  • Arithmetic and p-adic geometry
  • Motivic and syntomic cohomology
  • Poincaré bundles and Eisenstein classes
  • p-adic Fourier theory and distributions
  • Formal groups and deformation theory
  • Iwasawa theory and special values of L-functions

Current research

Selected directions

I am currently working on integral phenomena in p-adic geometry, arithmetic distributions and Hecke correspondences, and deformation-theoretic structures associated with formal groups and finite subgroup schemes.
