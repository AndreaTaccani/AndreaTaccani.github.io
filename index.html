Andrea Taccani

I am an incoming doctoral researcher in mathematics at the University of Regensburg. From October 2026, I will work under the supervision of Prof. Guido Kings.

I am currently completing my master's degree at the University of Padua during an Erasmus period in Regensburg. My interests lie in arithmetic geometry and algebraic number theory, with emphasis on geometric constructions that produce cohomology classes and p-adic distributions.

Research interests

  • Poincaré bundles and biextensions
  • Theta functions on elliptic curves
  • Eisenstein–Kronecker classes
  • Motivic and syntomic cohomology
  • Iwasawa theory and p-adic methods
  • Special values of L-functions

In progress · 2026
