Arithmetic geometry · Number theory

Andrea Taccani

I am an incoming doctoral researcher in mathematics at the University of Regensburg. From October 2026, I will work under the supervision of Prof. Guido Kings.

My interests lie in arithmetic geometry and algebraic number theory, particularly Poincaré bundles, Eisenstein–Kronecker classes, Iwasawa theory, and special values of L-functions.

Research interests

Geometry behind arithmetic phenomena

I am interested in how geometric constructions on elliptic curves and abelian varieties encode cohomology classes, p-adic distributions, and special values.

01

Poincaré geometry

Poincaré bundles, theta functions, biextensions, and their differential and functorial structures.

02

p-adic methods

Iwasawa theory, syntomic and étale realizations, and the behavior of arithmetic classes in p-adic families.

03

Special values

Eisenstein–Kronecker constructions, motivic cohomology, and links with L-functions.

Current work

Master's thesis

Poincaré Theta Distributions along Ordinary p-Power Isogenies

The thesis studies theta distributions arising from the Poincaré bundle along towers of ordinary p-power isogenies. It is being completed in 2026.
